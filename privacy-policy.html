BLASON CAR RENTAL · Version 1.1.0 · Effective 6 November 2026

01

Who we are and what this covers

BLASON CAR RENTAL provides an Android app for exploring five illustrative concept cars, estimating rental, fuel and excess-mileage costs in Moroccan dirhams and reading company information. This policy describes data handling by that app, version 1.1.0 (package ma.blason.blason_car_rental).

The app is an offline planning tool. It does not confirm vehicle availability, accept reservations, take payments or create rental agreements.

02

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal or device data. It does not request your name, email address, telephone number, identity documents, payment details or location.

There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking tools, crash-reporting services or third-party data-collection SDKs in the app. The app has no network functionality and makes no network requests. Its vehicle illustrations are generated in advance and bundled with the app; viewing them does not contact an image-generation service or send any prompt or data.

03

What happens to calculator inputs

The rental calculator uses your pickup and return dates, daily rental rate, daily extras and one-time charges. The fuel tool uses your planned distance, expected consumption and fuel price. The mileage tool uses your rental days, included kilometres per day, planned distance and excess-kilometre rate. These inputs are used only to calculate estimates on your device, remain in temporary app memory and are not sent to BLASON CAR RENTAL or any other party.

The app does not write those inputs to files, a database or persistent preferences. Clear amounts clears rental amounts; Clear fuel inputs and Clear mileage inputs clear the inputs in their respective tools. Rental dates can be changed in the calculator. Switching tabs or tools may discard inputs earlier. Ending the app process clears the temporary state; simply switching apps or pressing Back may leave the process in memory.

04

Permissions and device access

The Android app requests no device permissions, including internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, notifications or shared-storage access. It does not use an advertising identifier or read a device identifier.

The app uses standard on-device interface features, such as the keyboard and date/time picker. Those features do not send calculator values to the company.

05

Retention, security and deletion

There is no app user database or remote user-data service. Calculator values are held in temporary memory and may be discarded when navigating between screens or tools; they are not retained by the app after its process ends. Android app backup is disabled in this version.

Because the app has no accounts and does not send your inputs to us, we have no app account or server copy of those inputs to delete. You can clear amounts, end the app process or uninstall the app. We do not claim that these actions remove operating-system records, backups managed outside the app or communications you send separately.

06

Children's privacy

The app is intended as a rental-planning utility for adults and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from any user, including children. It does not enforce a rental eligibility or age check; actual rental requirements must be confirmed with the company.

07

Services outside the app

Your operating system, keyboard provider and app store may process information under their own terms and settings. The app does not control those independent services or receive their independent records.

This public policy webpage is separate from the Android app. Its HTML, CSS and JavaScript contain no analytics, advertising, cookies, browser storage or contact form. Loading a hosted webpage still sends ordinary technical request information, such as your IP address and browser headers, to the hosting service. That service may retain access or security logs under its own practices.

If you choose to email contact@blaston.com, your email provider, the company's email provider and the company receive the address, message and other information you include. Postal inquiries are also separate from the offline app. The app does not send these communications or collect that information. Avoid sending rental identity documents or payment details in a privacy inquiry.

08

Changes to this policy

This policy applies to the app version identified above. If app functionality or data handling changes, the policy and relevant store disclosures must be updated for that release. The effective date identifies the current policy version.
