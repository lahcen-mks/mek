Privacy Policy


At BLASON CAR RENTAL (SOCIETE A RESPONSABILITE LIMITEE — ICE 003141629000092, RC 128935, Souk Sebt Oulad Nemma (M), FQUIH BEN SALEH), your privacy matters. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you browse our website or make a reservation, how we use it, and the rights you hold under Moroccan Law 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data.

    Effective: September 2026 · FQUIH BEN SALEH, Morocco

    
1. Information We Collect

    
    
      
  • Identity & contact details you provide in the booking form: full name, phone/WhatsApp number, and email address.
    • 
      
  • Reservation details: chosen vehicle, pick-up and return dates, pick-up location, and any message or special request you send us.
    • 
      
  • Technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used solely to improve the website and diagnose errors.
    • 
      
  • Communications: messages exchanged with our team via WhatsApp, email, phone or social media in connection with your rental.
    • 
    


    
2. How We Use Your Information

    
    
      
  • To process, confirm and manage your car rental reservation.
    • 
      
  • To contact you about your booking (via WhatsApp, phone or email) and to provide roadside assistance when needed.
    • 
      
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations related to vehicle rental in Morocco (rental contracts, insurance, accounting).
    • 
      
  • To improve our services, fleet and website experience.
    • 
    


    
3. Booking via WhatsApp

    
When you confirm a reservation, the details you entered are composed into a message and sent by you through WhatsApp to our official number. That message is then processed under WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to this one. We use WhatsApp only to handle your request — never for unsolicited marketing without your consent.


    
4. Sharing & Disclosure

    
We never sell your personal data. Information is shared only with:

    
    
      
  • BLASON CAR RENTAL staff who need it to handle your reservation;
    • 
      
  • our insurance partners when required by your rental contract;
    • 
      
  • public authorities, when required by Moroccan law.
    • 
    

    
Payment details, if any, are handled by certified payment providers — we never store bank card numbers on our systems.


    
5. Data Retention

    
Reservation and contract records are kept for the legally required period for rental contracts and accounting purposes, then securely deleted or anonymised. General enquiries with no booking are deleted within 12 months.


    
6. Cookies

    
This website uses only essential technical storage strictly required for its functionality (for example, remembering your form inputs during a session). We do not run third-party advertising trackers or share browsing data with advertisers.


    
7. Your Rights (Law 09-08 & good practice)

    
Under Moroccan Law 09-08 — and as a matter of good practice — you may at any time:

    
    
      
  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
    • 
      
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
    • 
      
  • Delete your data where we have no legal obligation to keep it;
    • 
      
  • Object to processing, or request a copy of your data in a portable format.
    • 
    

    
To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We respond within 30 days.


    
8. Security

    
We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, loss or misuse. Access to reservation data is restricted to authorised staff only.


    
9. Minors

    
Our services are intended for individuals of legal driving age (21+ for most of our fleet, as stated in the rental contract). We do not knowingly collect data from minors.


    
10. Policy Updates

    
This policy may be updated from time to time; the latest version is always published on this page with its effective date. Material changes will be announced on the website home page.


    
11. Contact Us

    
Questions about this privacy policy or your personal data? Talk to us — we answer quickly.
